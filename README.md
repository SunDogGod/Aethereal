# Aethereal 3D

A third-person action game with a PS1 look, set in the Construct.

## Run it
1. Open Godot 4.7 and pick **Aethereal 3D** in the project list.
2. Press **F5** (or the ▶ button at the top right). The **title screen** opens: Continue, New Game, Chapters (any
   level), Settings, Quit. Arrows, WASD or the stick move; Enter, A or click choose; Esc or B goes back.
   The story runs on from level to level.
   To run one scene directly, open it and press **F6**.

Levels so far (rough cuts): 0.1 The Empty Town, 0.2 The Massacre, 0.3 Black Sand, 0.4 The Vacuum
Station. Their designs are in `docs/`.

## Isabella
She walks in Aeneas's footsteps (the exact path he took), a little to his side so she isn't
between him and the camera. Crouch and she crouches too and tucks in closer. Fall off the world
and she rejoins him. To add her to another level, drag `scenes/isabella.tscn` into it, then in the
Inspector set **Leader** to the Player.

## Sneaking (the Empty Town, from the ramp down)
- **Agents** see in a cone in front of them (the red eye-light shows where they look). Walls and
  railings block their view. Crouched, you're only seen at about half the distance.
- While an agent can see you, a **?** over his head fills from yellow to orange. When it's full he
  shouts **!**, and you go back to the last checkpoint.
- **Take down** an agent who isn't looking at you: get close and press F / X. If another agent would
  see it, he spots you, unless **Isabella** can reach him: then she binds him with ink at the same
  moment. The prompt at the bottom of the screen tells you which.
- **Checkpoints**: the bridge end, the rim, the top of the ramp, partway down the catwalk, the alley
  junction, and the courtyard. Being spotted, dying in a fight or falling off the town sends you back
  to the last one (SPOTTED / SLAIN / FELL). Agents you took down before it stay down. After the capture,
  restarts happen in the hall, and agents from fights you already won stay down. A level
  without a StealthDirector just puts you back on the last ground you stood on.
- **Drops**: jumping off a deck into the light well (any drop over 6 m) counts as a fall too: FELL,
  back to the last checkpoint. The height is **Fall Death Height** on the Player (Falling group).

To add an agent, drag `scenes/guard.tscn` into a level and turn him to face his post. To give him a
patrol, add a Node3D called **Route** under him with Marker3D points in it. He walks them in order.
His numbers (speed, sight distance, how fast he notices you) are in `data/enemies/agency_agent.tres`.
For a different kind of guard, make a new GuardData file instead of a new script.

## Fighting (Souls-style)
- Top left: health (red) and stamina (green). Attacks, dodges and blocked hits spend stamina;
  it refills when you stop for a moment.
- **Lock on** (Q) to face an enemy and strafe around him. A ◇ marks him.
- Enemies telegraph: the **visor flares red** and the blade rises before a swing.
- **Block** most of a hit (a quarter gets through). **Parry** by tapping block in the last
  moment of the flare: he reels, and your next attack is a riposte at three times the damage.
- **Dodge** (Space while locked on) is untouchable for its first third of a second.
- Enemy numbers (health, damage, windup, range) live in `data/enemies/*.tres`. A new kind of
  enemy is a new file, not a new script. A **Fight** node (`scripts/fight.gd`) groups the guards
  of one fight; a dialogue scene starts it with `[fight NodeName]`, and it plays a scene when won
  or when Aeneas dies.
- Story commands for fights: `[fight X]`, `[erupt on|off]` (the gravity eruption), `[empower X]`,
  `[kill X]` (a sacrifice, no blood), `[heal]`.
- Fighters **walk around things** to reach you (the stage, columns, the ramp rails), using the
  town's `Navigation` node, a navigation mesh over the courtyard and hall. If you move walls, ramps,
  columns or the stage there, select `Navigation` and click **Bake NavigationMesh** at the top of the
  3D view. Agents waiting for their story fight can't be hurt, locked onto or taken down early.

## Dialogue and cutscenes
- **The words** live in plain-text files in `data/dialogue/`, one per level (`empty_town.txt`).
  Open one in any text editor; the top of the file explains the format:
  `KAEL: Does anyone live here?` is a spoken line, a line with no name is narration,
  `[camera KaelShot]` cuts to a camera, `[camera]` cuts back, `[wait 1.5]` pauses,
  `[hide Kael]` / `[show Kael]` hide or show something in the level, `[fade out]` / `[fade in]` /
  `[fade white]`, `[title ACT 0 / BLACK SAND]` shows a title card, `[teleport MarkerName]` moves
  Aeneas to a Marker3D, and `[level res://scenes/levels/black_sand.tscn]` goes to another level.
- **Triggers** (the `StoryTriggers` node in a level) play a scene when Aeneas walks in. Each one
  names its dialogue file and scene in the Inspector.
- **Camera angles** are the Camera3D nodes under `Shots`. Select one and click **Preview** in the 3D
  view to see what it sees, then move it to reframe.
- While a scene plays, Aeneas can't move and guards don't look for him. Press Space, Enter, F or E
  (A, X or D-pad up on a gamepad) to finish a line, and again to go on.
- **Looking at things**: some objects show a prompt at the bottom of the screen (`E / Up: The well`).
  Press E or D-pad up and Aeneas's thoughts play as narration. The lines are in
  `data/inspect/empty_town.txt`, one per line: `NodeName|first line|second line`. To add a spot, copy an
  Interactable under the level's `Inspect` node, move it, name it, and add its line to the file.

## Tuning knobs (no code needed)
- **How Aeneas moves**: select `Player` in `player.tscn`; Running, Jumping and Crouching are in the Inspector.
- **How Isabella follows**: select `Isabella` in a level; distance, side offset, speed and reaction time are in the Inspector.
- **Vertex wobble**: Project → Project Settings → Shader Globals → `ps1_snap_resolution`. Lower = wobblier.
- **Texture warping**: open a material in `materials/` and change `Affine Amount` (1 = full PS1, 0 = off).
- **Fog / sky color / light**: the `WorldEnvironment` and `Sun` nodes in each level.
- **Screen color and dither**: `scenes/ps1_screen.tscn` → `Filter` → its material.
- **Sound**: ambient loops (neon hums, vending hums, the cube's drone) are AudioStreamPlayer3D nodes in
  the level: Volume Db, Unit Size (how far it stays at full volume) and Max Distance are in the
  Inspector. Footstep spacing and volume are on the Player's **Footsteps** node. One-shot volumes are the numbers in
  the `SfxBus.play(...)` calls. The WAVs are synthesized, so they are placeholders until real ones exist.
- **Life signs** (the `Life` node): flickering lights have a Personality (steady, buzzing, dying);
  TV windows a Brightness; steam vents and drips just sit where they are. Copy one to add another.

## The painted look
- Textures are 128 px, painted in code (`textures/*_painted.png`, `pavement.png`, `trim_sheet.png`,
  `vending.png`, `sign_glyphs.png`, decals). Soft colour variation, dark seams with a light lip, grime
  that pools at the bottom.
- **Vertex shading:** every Block darkens toward its foot (`Grime` and `Grime Height` in the Inspector),
  so walls look dirtier where feet and rain reach. Painted materials have `use_vertex_color` on.
- **Trim sheet** (`textures/trim_sheet.png`): eight strips (pipe, vent, cables, rail, warning stripes,
  rust, wet lip, neon tube). A Block with **Trim** on maps the strip along its faces; the `trim_*`
  materials pick the strip. Rails, pipes, AC units and neon tubes in the town use it.
- **Props** are plain Blocks under the town's `Dressing` node: vending machines, glyph signs, AC
  units, cable bundles, barrels, laundry. Copy one, move it, done. Decals (puddles, grime, posters)
  are under `Decals`.
- To change a sign's colour, swap its Plate's material between `sign_pink`, `sign_cyan`, `sign_amber`.
- **Buildings** are Blocks with **Style = Building** and a `building*` material. The faces are built
  as real fronts: a ground floor with doors (they don't open) and shuttered shopfronts, 3 m window
  bays with blank corners, a slab line per storey, a parapet and a plain roof. No windows on edges,
  roofs or undersides, and a face under 5 m wide or 4 m tall stays blank, so extrusions and ledges
  don't carry windows. The tiles come from `textures/building_atlas.png` (16 x 2 tiles of 64 px).
  Turn **Ground Floor** off for a block that floats above the street.

## Editing the gray-box
Every wall, floor and ramp in the Empty Town is a **Block**. Click one in the 3D view, then:
- Move or rotate it with the gizmo (W = move, E = rotate).
- Change **Size** in the Inspector; its collision follows automatically.
- Change **Material** by dragging a file from `materials/` onto it. `facade` = apartment walls with lit
  windows, `facade_dark` = the same, darker; `dev_grid` = the route; the `neon_*` ones glow.
- To add one, select a Block and press Ctrl+D to duplicate it.

Characters are built by the Blender scripts in `tools/blender/` (see its README; rebuild with `tools/blender/rebuild.ps1`). In the editor, the **Aethereal** dock adds guards, checkpoints, triggers, look-at spots, lights and props in one click; `docs/customizing.md` is the guide.
