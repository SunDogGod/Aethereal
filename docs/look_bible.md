# Look bible (a proposal, 2026-11-05)

Drawn from your picture folder (all 600 images read) and your inspirations, for the direction you gave: dark fantasy with a cyberpunk sci-fi aesthetic. Everything here is a proposal for you to keep or veto; the numbered list in section 3 is what the dressing round built from.


Dark fantasy on a cyberpunk skeleton. The short version: **the world is black, enormous, old and wired; every scene gets one hot colour; every sacred thing has an eye.** Your pictures say this over and over. The game already has the bones (the bridge approach, the hall bonfire, the Black Sand ceiling); what follows is how to push everything else toward those frames.

## 1. The five ideas your pictures keep repeating

**A tiny figure against a colossal machine.** Scale comes from emptiness and vertical lines, not detail: a ledge, a fog wall, one small person. Examples: `IMG_0138.JPG` (the falling figure between vertical bars), `Locations/IMG_1824.JPG` (the ring fan and cable bundles with a coated figure on a ledge), `IMG_5209.JPG` (the machine head hanging in a shaft of slab floors).

**One hot colour against near-black.** Almost every image you saved that works has a black or grey world and a single saturated note: a green river, an ember-edged wing, an orange eye slit. Examples: `Locations/IMG_1865.JPG` (acid-green river in a black city), `IMG_3506.JPG` (red-glowing wing edge over grey sand), `Items/IMG_2636.JPG` (black armour, orange slits, rust cloak).

**The black monolith.** A plain matte cube or slab in fog, with a thin cold light on one edge. It is the cheapest possible landmark in this art style and it reads as sacred technology. Examples: `06 Demiurge/_835742c1-7e71-4eec-b5e5-5d694aa0ddbc.jfif`, `IMG_1784.PNG` (the cube over kneeling crowds under grey cloud), `IMG_1113.JPG` (the steel slab in red rock).

**Eyes and halos as holy horror.** Rings, wheels and single eyes: a halo over a jaw of teeth, eyes in palms, a dome of cells each holding an eye. This is the game's cult mark waiting to be drawn. Examples: `IMG_2204.JPG` (the Pale Man), `08 Yellow Queen/1778360489922553.jpg` (the eye-lattice dome), `IMG_4625.JPG` (helmet, red eye, halo ring).

**The dead god wired into the machine.** Cables plugged into bodies, thrones with cable halos, verdigris metal with orange rust and one warm lamp. This is where dark fantasy and cyberpunk meet in a single object. Examples: `IMG_3332.JPG` (the corpse on the golden machine throne), `Aethereal10-2026/iCloud Photos/IMG_0002.PNG` (the Quake 4 operating chair), `IMG_5212.JPG` (the girl with the cable backpack).

A sixth that supports all five: **overgrown, flooded, verdigris** (`Locations/IMG_1815.JPG`, `IMG_1925.JPG`). Moss and water are how the megastructure shows its age.

## 2. Palette and light plan per level

**Empty Town.** Keep the slate concrete and the amber/cyan windows, but tame them: windows at half brightness, one storey in three dark, some runs blank or cracked, so the towers feel ancient and half-empty. Add a third accent at street level: **sodium orange** pools under lamps, and one pink neon source sign down the alley (not a free-floating glow). Sky: a thin magenta-violet dusk band at the horizon fading up to black-green (`Locations/IMG_1817.JPG`), with the cloud deck lit faintly from below by the city. Key light is cold from above (sky), warm pools below. The hall keeps its bonfire and gains a **violet** Aether note at the ritual circle, the game's single magic colour.

**Black Sand.** The violet-grey sand and black sky are right; the level is just missing a second light. Add **dull red beacons** on the rig masts, two sodium floods at the camp, and an amber ground glow under the far town so it sits on something. Skylights stop being white rectangles and become pale lilac cones of light with dust in them. The fog is mauve-grey haze, like `IMG_2137.PNG`. The one monolith landmark (below) gets a cold white edge.

**Vacuum Station.** Teal and navy stay, but the hall needs a warm counterpoint: **amber lamps** on the pillars, beige CRT terminals with red bar graphs (`IMG_1928-1.PNG`), verdigris and rust streaks down the white hull. The corridor's cyan floor tiles stay the cold note. The Grey dream gets the Evangelion sky: starry violet fading to a crimson horizon over a pale glossy floor (`IMG_4623.JPG`).

**Massacre.** This is the level that fights the brief. Desaturate everything: grass to olive-grey, roofs to dark clay, stucco to dirty cream. Then make the village a **warm lantern pool inside blue-black fog**, like the Sekiro hall (`IMG_4402.JPEG`): indigo shadows, amber light only where lanterns hang. The robes keep red, blue and gold but muted, so the lanterns are the only saturated thing. Fewer, dimmer ceiling panels; smoke haze; the structure walls fade into fog.

## 5. What to avoid

- **The old pixel art.** Silhouette ideas only; no designs copied.
- **Fine lines and thin glyphs.** They turn to noise at 960x540. Every sigil gets fat strokes.
- **Even grids and even brightness.** Regular windows at full glow make the towers look new.
- **More than one neon colour per block.** The Transmetropolitan carnival is fun, but the game's look is one hot note on black.
- **Rainbow chakra palettes.** Use seven-tier ideas as structure, never as colour.
- **Pastels outside dreams.** The train, the Grey dream, memories: yes. The megastructure: no.
- **Real-world cult and conspiracy material as canon.** Invent the alphabet and the names; the Hebrew, Latin and conspiracy text in the charts stays out.
- **Four files in the folder are hateful memes** (`IMG_2929.JPG`, `IMG_3485.JPG`, `IMG_3504.JPG`, `IMG_3501.JPG`). Nothing in them is a reference; I recommend removing them.
- **Real-time shadows and affine warp.** Chiaroscuro comes from placed lights and dark gaps, not shadow maps.
