# The Empty Town: the story as the author tells it (2026-10-26)

This supersedes the beats in `docs/empty_town.md` where they disagree. The author's own telling,
lightly cleaned up ("Sera" in the first line read as Isabella; see the note at the end):

> Aeneas and Isabella start the story on their way to visit the village for supplies from their
> house. They meet Kael, who is a stranger. The Agency has ransacked the town. You get introduced
> to Aether with Isabella's attack, but she has to say something like "You get the first one, I'll
> get the second." Then when they make it to the courtyard you see the Agency taking Valentina away
> in a cutscene. The cutscene ends and you fight off a few guys, killing them. All that's left is
> the guy holding Valentina. A new cutscene: Aeneas has him cornered, only for the lieutenant to
> sneak behind him with a knife to his throat, and we look to see that someone else has done the
> same to Isabella. They take all three into the hall, where there is a big bonfire in the middle
> where the bodies of the villagers are being thrown, and the cube has an aura around it showing
> that it is accepting the sacrifices. The lieutenant tells the captain that they missed a few and
> they found this kid after he had killed some of the men. The captain says that he's going to
> enjoy killing Aeneas. At this point Isabella uses her ink to break free from her restraints and
> kill the man holding her and a few other guards. She tries to kill the captain but he's too fast
> for her and he stabs her in the heart. She staggers and looks Aeneas in the eye, crying, tells
> him not to give up, that she loves him. She uses her paint to draw a circular symbol on the floor
> around her and she uses the cube to sacrifice her soul. She sacrifices her soul to release the
> seal she had placed on Aeneas's aether, allowing him to unlock his aether naturally, but she is
> killing two birds with one stone by awakening his aether for him. Since the sacrifice is so
> meaningful to them both, Aeneas unlocks a massive amount of raw aether and it explodes out of him,
> killing several men. Only the captain, the lieutenant and Valentina are alive. Aeneas kills the
> lieutenant easily, and the captain sacrifices Valentina and throws her into the fire for a power
> boost, defeats Aeneas and holds him up by the throat. He tells him that he sacrificed his right
> arm to make his left more powerful, and each villager made him stronger still, and since his left
> wasn't his dominant he got an extra boost. The captain doesn't use a sword; he fights with his
> fists. Aeneas gave him a run for his money, but now he's going to sacrifice Aeneas. Aeneas is
> about to black out when someone slices the captain's left and only arm. They both fall to their
> knees to see Kael there with his sword full of blood. He then beheads the captain, and just as
> Aeneas passes out he hears "Sacrificio".

## Rules from this telling
- **"Sacrificio"** is what they say when they begin a sacrifice (not "I sacrifice").
- A sacrifice only counts if the body is burned or given to the cube. The villagers' bodies are
  burned on the hall's bonfire; the cube's aura shows it accepting them. Isabella sacrifices her
  soul through the cube; her body stays (it lies later in the family shed in Black Sand).
- **The captain fights with his fists**, left arm only (he sacrificed his right to strengthen his
  left). No sword.
- The **lieutenant** carries a knife (he takes Aeneas hostage with it). Aeneas kills him.
- **Valentina** is sacrificed to the fire by the captain for a power boost, before the boss fight.
- The fight with the captain is lost: he holds Aeneas up by the throat; Kael cuts off his arm and
  beheads him. Updated 2026-10-26 (the author): Aeneas falls to the floor, the captain sinks to his
  knees, Kael says "Sacrificio" and beheads him on camera; a held second, a slow blackout and a slow
  fade into the Massacre memory.

## Beat order (for scenes and fights)
All eleven are built (2026-10-26); the table at the end of `docs/empty_town.md` says which scene or
fight plays each one.
1. Arrival with Isabella (supplies), meet Kael (stranger), the ransacked town (round-up).
2. Sneaking. At the first paired takedown Isabella says "You get the first one, I'll get the
   second." and her ink takes the second agent: the player's first sight of Aether.
3. Courtyard cutscene: the Agency taking Valentina away. Fight: kill the agents; the one holding
   Valentina stays out of it.
4. Cutscene: Aeneas corners him; the lieutenant's knife at Aeneas's throat; another agent has
   Isabella the same way. All three taken into the hall.
5. Hall: bonfire with villagers' bodies, the cube's aura. Lieutenant and captain lines. Isabella
   breaks free with ink, kills her captor and a few guards, goes for the captain, is stabbed in the
   heart. Her last words to Aeneas; the ink circle on the floor; "Sacrificio"; her soul to the cube;
   the seal on his aether released.
6. Aeneas's raw aether erupts and kills several men (playable eruption). Left: the captain, the
   lieutenant, Valentina.
7. Aeneas kills the lieutenant (easy fight).
8. The captain throws Valentina into the fire: "Sacrificio", power boost.
9. The captain fight (fists), choreographed; Aeneas gives him a run for his money and loses.
10. The captain holds him up by the throat; the arm speech; he begins to sacrifice Aeneas.
11. A blade takes the captain's arm; Aeneas falls to the floor, the captain to his knees; Kael with a
    bloody sword says "Sacrificio" and beheads him (held a second on screen); slow blackout, slow fade
    into the Massacre memory.
