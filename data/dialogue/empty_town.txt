# gave them); rewrite them in your voice. The game picks up changes the next time you press F5.

# How to write a scene:
#   === scene_name ===          starts a scene. A trigger in the level plays it by this name.
#   KAEL: Does anyone live here?
#                               a spoken line: the NAME in capitals, a colon, then the words.
#   The wind stops.             a line with no NAME is narration.
#   [camera KaelShot]           cut to a camera (a Camera3D under the level's Shots node).
#   [camera]                    cut back to the normal camera behind Aeneas.
#   [frame Captain]             cut to a side-on shot of Aeneas and the Captain, wherever they stand.
#   [wait 1.5]                  pause for 1.5 seconds.
#   [hide Kael] / [show Kael]   hide or show something in the level, by its node name.
#   [fade out] / [fade in]      to black and back; [fade dim] darkens the screen part way.
#   [collapse IsabellaHall]     a character falls and stays down (her death animation).
# Stage directions (who stands where and what they do; the markers are under StoryMarks):
#   [place Valentina DragStartV]     stand on a marker, facing the way it faces
#   [move Valentina DragEndV 4]      walk to a marker in 4 seconds (the scene carries on)
#   [stand Lieutenant Player -0.3]   0.3 m behind Aeneas, facing the same way (a plus number:
#                                    in front of him, face to face). "Player" is Aeneas.
#   [face IsabellaHall Player]       turn to look at someone
#   [pose IsabellaHall kneel_draw]   play a story pose (the list is in scripts/character_story.gd);
#   [pose IsabellaHall]              ...and with no pose, back to standing
#   [ink IsabellaHall HallGuard1 0.85]  her brush's ink takes that agent 0.85 s from now
#   [kill Captain]                   an agent drops where he stands, no blood
#   [call Bonfire throw_in ValentinaHall]  run a node's own action (here: the fire takes her)
#   [show CubeSurge] / [show CubePulse] / [show FireFlare]  the cube's aura surges or pulses; the fire leaps
# Lines starting with # are notes to yourself; the game ignores them.
