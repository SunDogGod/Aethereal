# THE EMPTY TOWN: every spoken scene in the level.
#
# DRAFT. These lines only restate the beats we designed together. Rewrite them in your voice;
# the game picks up changes the next time you press F5.
#
# How to write a scene:
#   === scene_name ===          starts a scene. A trigger in the level plays it by this name.
#   KAEL: Does anyone live here?
#                               a spoken line: the NAME in capitals, a colon, then the words.
#   The wind stops.             a line with no NAME is narration.
#   [camera KaelShot]           cut to a camera (a Camera3D under the level's Shots node).
#   [camera]                    cut back to the normal camera behind Aeneas.
#   [wait 1.5]                  pause for 1.5 seconds.
#   [hide Kael] / [show Kael]   hide or show something in the level, by its node name.
# Lines starting with # are notes to yourself; the game ignores them.

=== arrival ===
[camera OpeningShot]
ISABELLA: Remember, we're just making a quick stop into town today. No going off to flirt with Valentina.
AENEAS: Aw, c'mon, Mom. You know I'm going to marry her.
ISABELLA: Yes. But wait until this last harvest, so you can purchase a home in the city.
[camera TownShot]
AENEAS: That's strange. The town is awfully quiet.
[camera]

=== kael_meeting ===
[camera KaelShot]
KAEL: Hello. My name is Kael, and I'm just a visitor passing through.
AENEAS: A visitor? It's been years since we've seen anyone from outside the village.
KAEL: Yes. My village was attacked, and I've been wandering, looking for a home.
KAEL: This town shows signs of life, but I don't see anyone.
ISABELLA: That's impossible. This city is packed with people. But then again, we usually hear them from far away.
AENEAS: Let me check it out.
[camera]

=== round_up ===
[camera RoundUpShot]
Agency agents lead the villagers across the catwalk, down toward the hall.
Kael splits off from them.
[hide Kael]
[hide KaelTag]
[camera]

=== valentina ===
[camera ValentinaShot]
An agent drags Valentina toward the hall.
AENEAS: Valentina!
Aeneas breaks cover.

[camera]

=== captured ===
[camera ValentinaShot]
Valentina is free. For a moment.
[show Reinforcements]
Boots on every stair. A dozen agents, calm as clerks.
There is nothing left to do but be taken.
[fade out]
[hide Isabella]
[hide Valentina]
[hide ValentinaTag]
[hide Reinforcements]
[teleport HallMark]
[show ValentinaHall]
[show IsabellaHall]
[camera HallShot]
[fade in]
The hall. The villagers kneel in rows before a black cube. The captain stands over it.
CAPTAIN: Sacrificio.
One by one, the villagers go into the cube.
[hide HallVillagers]
[camera SacrificeShot]
Isabella stands. Nobody stops her. This is what they came for.
She draws. Ink hangs in the air: circles inside circles, symbols Aeneas has never seen.
[show InkCircle]
She rings the bell.
ISABELLA: I sacrifice.
[hide IsabellaHall]
[hide InkCircle]
And she is gone. Perfectly.
[camera EruptionShot]
Something opens behind Aeneas's eyes, and the floor forgets which way is down.
[camera]
[erupt on]
(The eruption: press F to slam everything around you. Nothing can hurt you.)
[fight HallFight]

=== captain_phase ===
[camera CaptainShot]
The agents are down. The captain steps off the stage, the lieutenant at his shoulder.
CAPTAIN: Raw. Untrained. Still worth collecting.
[erupt off]
[heal]
The power drains out of him as fast as it came. Only the sword is left.
[camera]
[fight CaptainFight]

=== lieutenant ===
[camera CaptainShot]
The captain is losing. He knows it.
He takes his lieutenant by the collar and walks him to the cube.
CAPTAIN: Sacrificio.
[kill Lieutenant]
A rushed, ugly offering, right after hers. The cube takes it anyway.
[empower Captain]
CAPTAIN: Again.
[camera]

=== valentina_death ===
[camera ValentinaDeathShot]
Aeneas is down. The captain drags Valentina between them.
Aeneas pulls the power back, so it won't crush her.
The captain kills her anyway, and brings the hilt down on Aeneas's skull.
[hide ValentinaHall]
[fade out]
A sword from nowhere. A stranger's voice. Kael.
