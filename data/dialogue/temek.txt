=== arrival ===
[camera CausewayShot]
# DRAFT
Temek stands in its lake: streets of stone on the water, derricks burning on every corner.
# DRAFT
And over the middle of it, hanging from the roof with its point down, the black pyramid.
[wait 2.0]
[camera]

=== the_market ===
[camera MarketShot]
# DRAFT
The market street is half shut. The beggars are not.
# DRAFT
The man in linen walks them through without slowing, toward his master's house on the left.
[camera]

=== the_nobleman ===
[camera CourtShot]
# DRAFT
The nobleman keeps a courtyard with a roof round it and shade in it, and he gives them both.
# DRAFT
He is one of the Pharaoh's advisors, and he asks about the road before he asks their names.
[camera PoolShot]
# DRAFT
His pool is dry. All the water in Temek is going the same way.
# DRAFT
The lake is sinking, and the priests have the answer they always have: the nobles and the Pharaoh will each give their firstborn.
[wait 1.5]
[camera CourtShot]
# DRAFT
When Aeneas tells him what came up through the sand and took the pipe, the nobleman goes quiet, and then he smiles. Something to blame is something to fix.
# DRAFT
He sends word to the palace across the water.
[camera]

=== the_pyramid ===
[camera UnderShot]
# DRAFT
Under the pyramid the people of Temek come one at a time to put a hand on it.
[camera UpShot]
# DRAFT
It is cold, and it goes up into the smog further than the eye can follow.
# DRAFT
They say it comes down a little with every sacrifice.
[wait 1.5]
[camera]

=== the_pharaoh ===
[camera PorchShot]
# DRAFT
The Pharaoh meets them on the steps of his own pyramid, with his guard behind him.
# DRAFT
He cannot stop the ceremony. His firstborn son is to be given with the others.
# DRAFT
What he can do is send soldiers: his best, to kill the centipede and mend the pipe before the day comes.
[camera KiraShot]
# DRAFT
And he sends his eldest daughter with them. Kira.
[wait 1.5]
[camera]

=== the_smith ===
[camera ForgeShot]
# DRAFT
The palace smith looks at the two halves of the sword for a long time.
# DRAFT
He doesn't mend it. He makes two daggers of it.
[wait 1.5]
[fade out]
# DRAFT
They leave by the north road at first light, with Kira and the Pharaoh's soldiers, back toward the pipe. (The rough cut ends here.)
